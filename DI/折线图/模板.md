# 折线图 Line Graph 模板

**模板**

1. This line graph shows changes in **[主题]** from **[起始年]** to **[结束年]**.
2. **[A]** rose steadily from **[x]** to **[y]**, reaching a peak in **[年]**.
3. **[B]**, on the other hand, declined gradually to about **[z]** by **[年]**.
4. The two lines crossed around **[年]**, when both were at roughly **[数字]**.
5. Overall, **[A]** shows an upward trend, while **[B]** shows a downward trend.

**要点：** 只讲起点、终点、最高点、交叉点；趋势词：rose / increased / fluctuated / remained stable / dropped / declined。

练过的题放在这个文件夹里，一题一个文件。
