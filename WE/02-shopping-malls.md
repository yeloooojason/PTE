# Shopping Malls Replacing Local Shops（2026-10-20）

**题目：** In many towns and cities large shopping malls are replacing small local shops. Some people say that this is a positive development. To what extent do you agree?

题型：to what extent。立场句用"I agree with this view to a large extent."

**我写的主体 1 原稿**

> Firstly, large shopping malls can have a positive effect on people's lives. This is because people are more likely to save more time on shopping when they are in the large shopping malls. For example, when people are shopping in the large mall, they can compare the products with difference brand. As a result, this may create more opportunities for them to decide which products they like and more choice for them to choose. Therefore, X can play an important role in ___.

**原稿修改**

| 原句 | 问题 | 改成 |
|---|---|---|
| are more likely to save more time on shopping | more 重复，句子绕 | can save a great deal of time when they do all their shopping in one place |
| in the large shopping malls / in the large mall | 泛指不用 the | in a large mall |
| compare the products with difference brand | difference 是名词；brand 要复数 | compare products from different brands |
| 例子只讲"比较品牌" | 和上一句"省时间"没对上 | 先说"一站买齐衣服、食品、电器"，再说比较品牌 |
| this may create more opportunities … and more choice for them to choose | choice … to choose 重复；this 指代不清 | they have more choice and can decide which products suit them best |
| X can play an important role in ___ | 模板占位没填 | large shopping malls can play an important role in making shopping more convenient and efficient |

**参考范文（282 词：60 / 94 / 86 / 42）**

> In recent years, large shopping malls have increasingly replaced small local shops in many towns and cities. While some people believe that this is a positive development, others argue that it damages the character of local communities. I agree with this view to a large extent, and this essay will discuss the convenience and the economic benefits that malls bring.
>
> Firstly, large shopping malls can have a positive effect on people's lives. This is because people can save a great deal of time when they do all their shopping in one place. For example, in a large mall, shoppers can buy clothes, groceries and electronics under one roof, and they can easily compare products from different brands. As a result, they have more choice and can decide which products suit them best without travelling from shop to shop. Therefore, large shopping malls can play an important role in making shopping more convenient and efficient.
>
> Furthermore, shopping malls can benefit the local economy. Specifically, a large mall brings together hundreds of shops, restaurants and cinemas, which creates many jobs for local residents. A good illustration of this is Westfield in Sydney, which employs thousands of people and attracts visitors from other suburbs every weekend. Consequently, more money is spent in the area, and the town becomes more lively. Although some small shops may close because they cannot compete, many owners can rent space inside the mall and continue their businesses there.
>
> In conclusion, I largely agree that the replacement of small shops by large malls is a positive development because malls save shoppers time and support the local economy. Therefore, local governments should encourage mall developers to offer affordable spaces to small businesses.

**这篇的要点**

- to what extent 题只选一边（大部分同意），两个主体段都是支持的理由，让步放在主体 2 的 Although 句里，不要单独写一段反方。
- 题目关键词 large shopping malls / small local shops / positive development 在开头、主题句、结尾都出现。
- 句式：which 定语从句（主体 2 两处），Although 让步句，while 对比句。
- 例子要和主题句对上：主体 1 讲省时间，例子就要体现"一个地方买齐"；主体 2 讲经济，例子就要有就业和消费。
