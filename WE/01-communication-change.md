# Communication Change: Positive and Negative Impacts（2026-10-20）

**题目：** Communication has changed significantly in the last 10 years. Discuss the positive and negative impacts of this change.

题型：利弊（讨论正面和负面影响）。立场句用"Although … has some drawbacks, I believe its advantages outweigh them."

**参考范文（278 词：60 / 89 / 85 / 44）**

> In recent years, the way people communicate has changed dramatically because of smartphones and social media. While some people believe that this change has made communication faster and more convenient, others argue that it has damaged face-to-face relationships. Although it has some drawbacks, I believe its advantages outweigh them, and this essay will discuss both its positive and negative impacts.
>
> To begin with, the main positive impact is that people can now communicate instantly and cheaply across long distances. This is because apps such as WhatsApp and WeChat allow users not only to send messages but also to make video calls for free. For example, international students in Australia can see and talk to their families at home every day, which was expensive and difficult ten years ago. As a result, distance is no longer a serious barrier to close relationships. Therefore, modern communication has brought people closer together.
>
> However, this change has also had negative impacts, particularly on face-to-face interaction. Specifically, many people spend so much time on their phones that they pay less attention to the people around them. A good illustration of this is a family dinner where everyone is checking social media instead of talking. Consequently, real conversations become shorter and less meaningful. Although these problems are serious, they are caused by how people use technology rather than by the technology itself, so they can be reduced through better habits.
>
> In conclusion, the change in communication over the last decade has been largely beneficial because it makes contact fast and affordable, even though it can reduce face-to-face interaction. Therefore, individuals should use digital tools wisely, for example by putting their phones away during meals.

**这篇的要点**

- 题目关键词 communication / changed / last 10 years / positive / negative impacts 在开头和两个主体段主题句里都出现了（ten years ago、over the last decade 是同义替换），内容分靠这个。
- 主体 2 讲的是**负面**，所以把模板的 Furthermore 换成 However，转折更自然；模板里的其他连接词照用。
- 模板要求的句式都有：not only … but also …（主体 1），which 定语从句（主体 1），Although 让步句（开头、主体 2），so … that …（主体 2）。
- 例子都具体：WhatsApp、WeChat、留学生、家庭聚餐，不写空话。
- 没有缩写、问句、感叹号；拼写统一（全文没有英美拼写差异的词）。
