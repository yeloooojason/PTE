# 饼图 Pie Chart 模板

← 返回 [DI 总览](../README.md)

## 基本模板

1. This pie chart shows the proportion of **[整体]** **[in 年份/地点]**.
2. **[A]** accounts for the largest share, at **[x]** percent.
3. It is followed by **[B]**, which makes up **[y]** percent.
4. **[C]** and **[D]** each account for around **[z]** percent, while **[E]** is the smallest, at only **[w]** percent.
5. Overall, **[A]** and **[B]** together make up more than half of the total.

## 要点

两个饼图时先说第一个，再用 "In comparison, in [年], …" 说变化最大的一项。
