## 柱状图 Bar Chart

**模板**

1. This bar chart compares **[对象]** in terms of **[数值项]** **[in 年份/among 国家]**.
2. **[A]** has the highest **[数值项]**, at about **[数字+单位]**.
3. **[B]** comes second, with around **[数字]**.
4. In contrast, **[C]** has the lowest, at only **[数字]**.
5. Overall, **[A]** is far ahead of the others, while **[C]** lags behind.

**要点：** 先看纵轴单位；有多组颜色时补一句 "In most categories, [颜色/组] is higher than [另一组]."

## 折线图 Line Graph

**模板**

1. This line graph shows changes in **[主题]** from **[起始年]** to **[结束年]**.
2. **[A]** rose steadily from **[x]** to **[y]**, reaching a peak in **[年]**.
3. **[B]**, on the other hand, declined gradually to about **[z]** by **[年]**.
4. The two lines crossed around **[年]**, when both were at roughly **[数字]**.
5. Overall, **[A]** shows an upward trend, while **[B]** shows a downward trend.

**要点：** 只讲起点、终点、最高点、交叉点；趋势词：rose / increased / fluctuated / remained stable / dropped / declined。

## 饼图 Pie Chart

**模板**

1. This pie chart shows the proportion of **[整体]** **[in 年份/地点]**.
2. **[A]** accounts for the largest share, at **[x]** percent.
3. It is followed by **[B]**, which makes up **[y]** percent.
4. **[C]** and **[D]** each account for around **[z]** percent, while **[E]** is the smallest, at only **[w]** percent.
5. Overall, **[A]** and **[B]** together make up more than half of the total.

**要点：** 两个饼图时先说第一个，再用 "In comparison, in [年], …" 说变化最大的一项。

## 流程图 Flowchart / Process

**模板**

1. This flowchart shows the process of **[主题/标题]**, which consists of **[n]** main stages.
2. It begins with **[第一步]**.
3. Then **[第二步]**, followed by **[第三步]**.
4. After that, **[分叉：the process splits into two branches: A and B]** / **[下一步]**, and both lead to **[汇合步骤]**.
5. Finally, **[最后一步]**. Overall, the diagram shows how **[东西]** moves step by step from **[起点]** to **[终点]**.

**要点：** 每个框都要说到；顺序词轮着用 begins with → then → followed by → after that → finally；循环图结尾说 "and the cycle starts again"。

**示例**

### 1. Food Supply Chain（2026-10-14）

![Food Supply Chain](images/di/flowchart-food-supply-chain.png)

图：Farming → Factory → Transportation → 分成 Retail 和 Services 两支 → Consumer → Recycling。

**参考答案（约 75 词，正常语速约 35 秒）**

> This flowchart shows the food supply chain, which consists of six main stages. It begins with farming. The products are then processed in factories and delivered by transportation. After that, the chain splits into two branches: retail stores and food services, such as restaurants. Both branches lead to consumers, who buy and eat the food. Finally, the waste goes to recycling. Overall, the diagram shows how food moves step by step from farms to our tables.

**这张图的要点**

- 流程图没有数字，内容分靠**把每个标签都说出来**（六个框一个不漏）和**说清顺序**。
- 顺序词轮着用：begins with → then → after that → finally。
- 分叉处是亮点：the chain splits into two branches，再用 both branches lead to 合回来。
- 结尾一句总结整个过程，不要说 "this image is very informative"。

## 地图 Map

**模板**

1. This map shows the layout of **[地点]** **[in 年份]**.
2. **[A]** is located in the north of the area, next to **[B]**.
3. In the centre, there is **[C]**, which is surrounded by **[D]**.
4. To the south / east, **[E]** lies along **[路/河]**.
5. Overall, the area is divided into **[几个区域]**, with **[主要设施]** in the middle.

**要点：** 方位词：in the north/south, next to, opposite, between, along；两张地图对比时用 "was replaced by / was built / was removed"。

## 照片 / 示意图 Picture

**模板**

1. This picture shows **[人/物]** **[在做什么]** in **[地点]**.
2. In the foreground, there is / are **[前景]**.
3. In the background, I can see **[背景]**.
4. **[人物]** seems to be **[动作/情绪]**, and the colours are mainly **[颜色]**.
5. Overall, the picture gives an impression of **[场景总结：a busy city / a peaceful village]**.

**要点：** 没有数据，就按"前景 → 背景 → 细节 → 感受"的顺序说；说看得见的东西，不要编。

## 组合图 Mixed

2. According to the **[图1]**, **[A]** has the highest **[数值]**, at **[数字]**.
3. **[B]** is the lowest, at **[数字]**.

5. Overall, both charts show that **[一个结论]**.
